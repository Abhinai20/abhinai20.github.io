AI Rephraser

Paste text and click "Rephrase" for an instant, fluent rewrite that fixes typos and grammar, then click any word in the result to swap in an alternative or revert it. Unlike every other tool on this site, your full pasted text is sent off-device to rewrite it (via a small proxy, then Google’s Gemini API) — word-swap clicks afterward only ever send the single word clicked.

Click "Rephrase" for an instant fluent rewrite — every word in the result will be clickable for alternatives.
